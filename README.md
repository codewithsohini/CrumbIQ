# CrumbIQ - AI-Powered Cafeteria Intelligence System

# Overview

CrumbIQ is an AI-powered cafeteria intelligence system designed to help educational institutions and cafeterias reduce food waste, optimize food preparation, and improve operational efficiency through predictive analytics. The system analyzes attendance patterns, demand trends, and waste data to generate intelligent recommendations that support better decision-making in cafeteria management.

# Problem Statement

Food waste is a major challenge in cafeterias and educational institutions. Traditional food preparation methods often rely on rough estimations, leading to:
->Overproduction of food
->Increased operational costs
->Resource wastage
->Environmental impact
->Difficulty in demand planning

CrumbIQ addresses these challenges by leveraging Artificial Intelligence and data-driven forecasting.

# Solution
CrumbIQ provides predictive insights that help cafeteria managers estimate food demand more accurately and make informed operational decisions.
The platform combines attendance forecasting, waste prediction, financial analysis, and sustainability metrics into a single dashboard.

# Key Features
📈 Attendance Forecasting:
Predicts expected student turnout based on historical attendance patterns.

🍛 Food Demand Prediction:
Estimates the quantity of food required for upcoming meal sessions.

♻️ Waste Prediction:
Forecasts potential food waste and identifies opportunities for reduction.

💰 Financial Impact Analysis:
Calculates estimated savings and losses associated with food preparation decisions.

🤖 AI-Powered Recommendations:
Provides intelligent suggestions for improving cafeteria operations and reducing waste.

⏰ Rush Hour Intelligence:
Identifies peak serving periods to assist in resource allocation and planning.

🌱 Sustainability Metrics:
Tracks environmental impact and food waste reduction efforts.

📊 Interactive Dashboard:
Visualizes key performance indicators through charts and analytics.
